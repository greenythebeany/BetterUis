What BetterUis is

BetterUis is a free, unofficial browser extension that changes how the STU university information system (UIS, is.stuba.sk) looks. It only changes the appearance of pages in your own browser. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (STU) or the information system's vendor.

Use at your own risk

BetterUis is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. Every form, button and link on is.stuba.sk keeps doing what the site itself does, but a restyled page can look different from the original. Before anything important - registering for exams, enrolling, submitting work, confirming documents - check the result; if anything looks wrong, switch the extension off and use the original site.
